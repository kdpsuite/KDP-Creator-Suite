FAQ

I got logged out between kdpsuite.com and the dashboard
Sign in once on dashboard.kdpsuite.com (canonical host — www.dashboard redirects there). Sessions use .kdpsuite.com cookies when Auth redirect URLs include both marketing and dashboard hosts. If it still fails, clear site data for both hosts and sign in again.
Convert or batch fails / quota errors
Free tier has monthly limits. Check Settings for usage, or open a support ticket / email with your account email and the error message.
Billing / invoices / cancel
After a paid Checkout (or any Stripe customer on file), use Settings → Manage billing for invoices, payment method, and cancel at period end. That opens Stripe’s Customer Portal. For Checkout failures, email support with the session id if you have it.
Upgrade to Pro / Studio
Free accounts: use Upgrade / Choose plan in the dashboard. Checkout runs on Stripe; promotion codes are supported when enabled on the session.
How fast do you reply?
Typical reply within 1–2 business days. Include account email and what you tried.

Open a ticket (signed in)

Use Dashboard → Settings → Support ticket while signed in. Tickets are stored against your account for follow-up. You can also email support@kdpsuite.com.
